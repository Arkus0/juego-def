## Programme before geometry

Freeze room/use/occupancy sketches and access graph before rebuilding. For every semantic building record identity, cell mapping, contiguous footprint/volume, public/private/service doors, visual floors, playable floors and availability. All 4–7 buildings have concrete use, including closed ones.

The block must include:

1. **Commerce:** customer area, usable witness/service position and useful stock/service relation; local GC2 examination/interaction suffices, no shopping system required.
2. **Housing:** a believable dwelling with living/domestic function, privacy and actual player/NPC scale; not another shop template behind a portal.
3. **Social venue or equivalent:** bar, café, association/local vecinal or another defensible place to stay/converse, with occupied seating/activity and passage that can coexist.
4. **One multistorey semantic building:** at least two playable floors connected by real usable stairs/landing; exterior may show more floors with honest limits.
5. **One deeper programme:** at least three activity-bearing rooms/zones across the multistorey building, supporting one small access/search/return exercise. This demonstrates mission-capable space, not a final mission or universal deep interiors. It may combine housing/social/commerce where coherent.
6. **Patio/rear/service space when morphology allows:** K2 must be surveyed for it. Prefer one useful court/rear access within the block. If physically impossible without losing preserved street/water character, record plan/section proof and a deliberate alternative service/rear arrangement. Omission without proof is FAIL; do not invent a tiny unused patio for the checklist.

At least **three distinct accessible programmes** (commerce, dwelling and social) operate, possibly in mixed buildings. At least **six useful rooms/activity zones** total, excluding corridors, stairs, vestibules, decorative rooms, simulated glass and duplicated activity markers. Each contributes a spatially distinguishable action/occupation. Some buildings must remain truthfully closed if that best serves the block; opening all is not an acceptance goal.

Exterior historical rhythms stay in FacadeCells: palette, weathering, roof/eaves variation, openings, setbacks and storey changes. Functional interior partitions may cross old cell lines; remove redundant party walls/colliders within a merged programme. New deep volumes must sit inside the block without intersections, impossible roofs or fake unowned voids.

## Radical utility acceptance, without metric gaming

Measure baseline and V2 within the same frozen boundary. `A` = net accessible activity-bearing interior area at real scale, excluding walls, circulation-only floor, blocked furniture, fake rooms and unsupported floor. `U` = distinct usable action/occupation locations, with programme and runtime receipt; duplicated markers, three counters for the same action or cosmetic doors are not new utility.

Mandatory floors of evidence: 3 accessible programmes, ≥6 useful rooms/zones, one ≥2-playable-floor building, one ≥3-room deeper programme, useful rear/court or proved morphological exception, 100% identity/space intent and full runtime proof.

Additionally require `A_V2 >= max(120 m², 2 * A_REF)` and `U_V2 >= max(6, 2 * U_REF)`. The absolute floors prevent a zero-baseline ratio from being called radical improvement. The ratios prevent an already functional baseline from passing on a tiny incremental gain. Area alone never suffices: each counted space passes occupancy/camera/access tests and no extra dead floor may be added to reach 120 m². Reviewer verifies area from geometry/programme, not the label count. No mandated city-wide accessibility percentage follows from this deliberately rich pilot.
