## Claim

juego-def can realize the accepted **B0 Mercado–Muelle** product brief as one actual keeper block using the accepted graphical/content factories. The result must feel like authored third-person game space and become the first retained piece of the shipping city.

This WP is the juego-def successor to the useful part of Juego2 CITY-07: **keeper game-space realization**. It deliberately does not import the old inland pilot geometry, H1/H2F prerequisites or CITY-04 remeasurement machinery.

## Binding city brief

Consume [`../design/FIRST_KEEPER_BLOCK_B0.md`](../design/FIRST_KEEPER_BLOCK_B0.md).

The semantic anchors and route roles are binding product intent:

- `L` lodging/return anchor;
- `A` market court/activity;
- `S` everyday shop + witness threshold;
- `E` commercial corner + port reveal;
- `P` public port threshold outside controlled work yard;
- `Q` quay overlook;
- `O` raised observation/chase choice;
- `V` quieter/stepped upper landing;
- direct commercial-to-port route plus genuine upper/port loops;
- honest expansion seams toward arrival, Casco, Viviendas and deeper Muelle/Talleres.

Starting coordinates/width bands are measurable hypotheses, not immutable shipping coordinates. Local geometry may be tuned through actual third-person realization when the functional roles, access truth and loops remain intact.

## Required block composition

At minimum:

1. B0 direct route and both useful cycle families are physically legible;
2. several coherent building/frontage outputs from ENV factory;
3. lodging/return anchor reads spatially;
4. market court/activity area reads as an ordinary public place;
5. shop/witness public threshold + shallow interior/edge works and service access remains distinct;
6. working-port reveal and public quay edge are readable without turning controlled work space into a shortcut;
7. raised/alternative route provides observation/follow/chase choice;
8. at least one strong working-port/Atlantic landmark cue;
9. **8+** generated civilians present in the block;
10. several NPCs consuming shared ambient/conversation/work animation vocabulary;
11. one investigation-style dialogue using accepted Dialogue/UI factory;
12. one additional world interaction/examinable;
13. enough prop/signage/material/lighting treatment to judge the block as retained game content.

## Keeper game-space realization — migrated CITY design rules

The block may not be an extruded plan or a dressing exercise. Perform a deliberate third-person design pass covering:

### Route rhythm

- compression -> expansion -> reveal;
- readable junctions and destinations;
- genuine cycles rather than arbitrary shortcuts;
- route learning through storefronts, water/work cues, elevation and landmarks;
- ordinary fabric between authored moments.

### Views + staging

- framed views and landmark emphasis;
- occlusion that stages discovery without destroying orientation;
- nooks/threshold pockets for conversations, waiting and observation;
- spatial support for following/searching and a bounded chase choice around the port/upper loop;
- future confrontation affordance without turning the block into an arena.

### Vertical coherence

- one coherent local elevation frame;
- intentional relation among street surfaces, stairs/ramps, retaining edges, thresholds and interior floors;
- no arbitrary per-building Y truths;
- elevation changes should improve route character/views while remaining comfortable and readable during walking/follow/chase.

### Threshold/access truth

- doors/interiors read from composition before UI;
- public/service/private roles remain visually and physically coherent;
- shop service access never becomes a fake public shortcut;
- public quay remains distinct from controlled port work space;
- scenic expansion seams must not masquerade as traversable roads.

## Factory integrity rule

The block must be produced **from the factories**, not by bypassing them with one-off hand work.

If integration exposes a systemic problem, repair/reopen the owning factory instead of hiding it locally:

- environment composition/kit issue -> ENV;
- character generation/clone/clipping issue -> CHAR;
- runtime animation/retarget issue -> ANIM;
- dialogue authoring/UI reuse issue -> DIALOGUE/UI.

Block-specific exceptions are allowed only when genuinely local and documented.

## Physical/product validation

Required:

- owner third-person walk-through;
- automated route/collision probe;
- direct and alternate route traversals;
- width/grade/threshold sanity checks for the realized candidate;
- NPC/world interaction in the actual block;
- no blocking runtime errors;
- gameplay captures from multiple relevant views including commercial approach, port reveal/quay and upper/alternate route;
- owner verdict that the block is worth **keeping and extending**, not discarding as a pipeline demo.

Measured gameplay truth comes from this realized candidate. Do not inherit inland CITY timings or claim provisional B0 diagram distances as measured play time.

## Production evidence

Record which assets were generated/selected through each factory and whether integration required exact-path owner hints, manual low-level fixes, new one-off tooling or factory extensions.

The desired result is that most work is selection/composition/content authoring, not foundational pipeline repair.

## PASS

PASS when:

- B0 Mercado–Muelle functional roles and loops survive realization;
- the block looks and plays like a credible early piece of the intended game;
- environment, civilian, animation and UI outputs all come through accepted factories;
- route rhythm, views, thresholds and elevation have received deliberate third-person composition rather than plan extrusion;
- public/service/private and public-port/controlled-work boundaries remain truthful;
- 8+ civilians coexist without obvious systemic clone/rig/material failures;
- investigation/world interactions work in Play Mode;
- route/collision/physical validation passes or real defects are corrected;
- expansion seams remain available without faking future traversable content;
- owner accepts the block as keeper content;
- no foundational factory blocker remains hidden behind block-specific fixes.

## FAIL

FAIL if the block bypasses factories, abandons the accepted B0 roles without product reason, still looks like dressed greybox, reads as a planning diagram rather than authored game space, service/controlled areas become fake shortcuts, elevation is incoherent, integration reveals unresolved systemic lane failures, or it becomes a one-off showcase that cannot seed the next district/content block.

## Non-goals

No full district, full living-world schedule system, final population count, combat or complete story slice. No resurrection of old inland CITY exact geometry or H1/H2F governance.
