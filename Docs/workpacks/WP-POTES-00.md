The casco is **not all Potes** and is **not the previous ENV01 procedural district**.

## Whole-map production thesis — binding

The intended final town is intentionally asymmetric:

| Zone | Exterior buildings | Spatial character | Primary gameplay |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| **Potes historic core** | **~55–65** | dense / vertical / urban | investigation, dialogue, politics, commerce, NPC routines, compact minigames |
| **Market / civic expansion** | ~10–20 | medium/open | events, crowds, commerce, social systems |
| **Residential expansion** | ~10–20 | open | home routines, substories, quieter traversal |
| **Port + workshops / industrial expansion** | ~10–20 | very open | combat, workshops, large interiors, jobs, port activity |

These later counts are planning envelopes, not implementation authority in this WP.

The historic core should carry roughly **40–60% of the final game's authored gameplay/activity weight**, despite occupying a smaller share of final traversable area.

Do **not** enlarge the casco to solve gameplay-space needs that belong in later open zones.

## Hard casco envelope

The exact selected perimeter must contain:

- target: **55–65 real exterior building footprints**;
- acceptable band: **50–70** only when complete real block/edge geometry makes 55–65 materially worse;
- **hard cap: 75 real exterior buildings** without explicit Owner amendment;
- one continuous, coherent Potes place;
- at least **two, preferably three, natural outward expansion interfaces**;
- a compact edge-to-edge third-person traversal target of roughly **2–4 minutes** before later expansions exist.

### Critical 1:1 rule

The building count is controlled by moving the **outer perimeter**, not by changing the real fabric inside it.

Forbidden merely to hit the count:

- merging two or more real exterior buildings into one;
- splitting a real exterior building;
- deleting an interior building;
- widening or shrinking a footprint;
- inventing a replacement building;
- moving doors/windows/roofs because the ENV kit fits better.

If a first cut contains too many buildings, trim at a defensible real seam: river, street, full block edge, parcel/back edge, slope transition or another visible urban boundary.

## What 1:1 means

For the initial exterior reconstruction, real Potes is factual authority for recoverable observable facts:

- street alignment, width and length;
- building footprint and orientation;
- relative elevation/grade;
- party-wall/adjoining condition;
- facade width and visible massing;
- storey count when recoverable;
- roof topology/orientation when recoverable;
- door/window/opening placement when observable;
- balconies, galleries, arcades and major attachments;
- walls, gates, stairs, railings and parcel boundaries;
- structural vegetation;
- river, bridge, plaza and retaining relationships;
- identity-bearing fixed public-space elements.

ENV01 supplies reusable pieces and production tooling. ENV01 does **not** decide the spatial composition.

Interior plans are not assumed 1:1 unless independently known. Exterior building != final gameplay interior programme.

- reproduce principal door position;

- reproduce major balconies/galleries/arcades;

Transient clutter may later use ENV dressing, but may not redefine routes, thresholds or reference-view identity.
