## Operative interpretation

The reference supports a hand-authored game environment. It does not require a
complete facade survey, rectified camera solution, millimetric stair survey or
forensic coverage of every inaccessible return. Earlier Worker reports demanded
too much metric completeness for this intended production use.

Preserve the unchanged footprints, individual body identities, adjoining fabric,
street connections, major massing and roof silhouette, playable level differences,
principal access positions and observed facade opening rhythms. Do not randomize
these, move them to fit ENV modules or reconstruct an ENV procedural district.

Use sourced **VISUAL_ESTIMATE** / **DERIVED** controls where they suffice for the
game's structure. A photographically estimated opening station is not a metric
survey. A roof-height distribution is not a recovered ridge. Unseen details remain
UNKNOWN in the factual ledger; their existence must not be fabricated or passed
to the visual Worker as an obligation to undertake research.

The Worker must instead supply a bounded, explicit construction instruction for
each material surface. If a missing fact changes a route, principal entrance,
major roof/annex silhouette, street facade rhythm or public level connection, it
still needs a control or an explicit game-reconstruction estimate here. Hidden
pane segmentation, individual shutter thickness, centimetric threshold offsets,
camera calibration and inaccessible secondary details do not block this structural
handoff by themselves.

The visual Worker consumes these instructions and authors the geometry manually.
This clarification does not start Unity reconstruction, select WP-POTES-01, enlarge
the core, authorize body deformation, buy dependencies, issue independent PASS or
authorize merge.
