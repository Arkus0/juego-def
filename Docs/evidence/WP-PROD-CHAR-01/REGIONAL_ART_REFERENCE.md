## Observed references

- [Oviedo sidrería group, La Nueva España](https://www.lne.es/oviedo/2024/09/22/ultima-sidra-leones-llevamos-buenos-108410899.html): different ages and face proportions in one ordinary group; closed smiles lift cheeks and compress the lower lid. Ordinary open shirts, service pockets, knit and a waistcoat have room around the torso.
- [Candás market portrait, La Nueva España](https://www.lne.es/centro/2013/10/20/pescado-fresco-trae-cola-candas-20549660.html): a broad friendly face, glasses, side-parted everyday hair and a soft jacket/scarf. The smile changes mouth width and cheek shape together.
- [Muros de Nalón fish seller, La Nueva España](https://www.lne.es/asturias/2025/04/10/pescadera-arte-muros-nalon-116228318.html): lively smile, raised cheeks, relaxed eyes and a simple loose crew-neck sweatshirt. The reference is expression and garment construction, not a copied person.
- [Liérganes market, Alerta](https://www.eldiarioalerta.com/articulo/trasmiera/lierganes-cantabria-uno-mejores-mercados-semanales-cantabria-pintoresco-pueblo/20250220172017552468.html): everyday mixture of shirts under knitwear, denim jackets, practical bags, heavier and slimmer silhouettes. Contemporary event clothing is filtered through B0's late-1990s/early-2000s setting.
- [Santander market and service portraits by Víctor Gómez, machbel](https://machbel.com/viajes/espana/cantabria/santander/): smiling fish seller with cheek/eye agreement and a working apron over loose sleeves; service staff show distinct grooming and constructed bib aprons.

These references are people photographed in the specified places. They do not establish one regional face type. Preserve varied age, build and features; avoid reducing the town to a stereotype.
