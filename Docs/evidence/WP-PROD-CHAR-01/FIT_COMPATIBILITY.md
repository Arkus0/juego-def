# Recipe compatibility and observed bad cases

These are Worker observations, not an independent PASS. The executable compatibility matrix is `Docs/asset_catalog/char_compatibility.json`. Fifteen exact body/outfit combinations have bounded idle/walk/talking evidence; untested combinations remain authoring candidates.

Current October 2 correction uses CC0 anatomical heads on the retained rig. Exact supporting-triangle weights from the complete, unmasked throat replace a generic Head/Neck ramp or disconnected visible skin. Closed shirts retain a 20 mm skin/facing overlap and mask hidden clavicle skin; the facing excludes shoulder/arm ray hits. Turned collars replace the upright jaw-crossing tube. Vests follow the supporting neckline; open edges are rounded within 6 mm and receive continuous shared-corner bindings. Facing measurements follow the retained throat after the concealed clavicle crop. Apron/overall ribbons are cut from the supporting shirt with matching weights and final neckline fit. New vertices inherit the original weighted surface, excluding degenerate triangles. These are shared rules, never hand-edited exports.

`negative_cases.json` records 59 executable rejections, including body/hair/profile/head sex, bundle license/pin/SHA/hair mismatch, unsafe IDs, wrong vest layer, invalid palettes, unsupported wardrobe, impossible proportions/presentation and empty batches. Source hashes and 65-bone donor rest checks fail before export on incompatible inputs. Unity rejects missing models, Avatars, bones and unknown material slots.

Current fit observation: retained front/rear/side views show attached hands/feet/head, functioning finger motion during Talking, stable footwear, and no severe body-through-cloth or broken limb deformation in the sampled poses. The apron now stays legible in walking front/side views; its single sheet has limited side volume and is a known visual constraint. Clothing is intentionally stylized and low-detail. Four samples of each clip do not admit arbitrary extreme poses, contacts, long skirts, animation blending or cloth collision. Any later observed clipping reopens the **combination/family**; it must not be waived because the current numeric checks are green.
