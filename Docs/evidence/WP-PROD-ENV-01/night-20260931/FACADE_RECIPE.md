# Lote B — recipe from the actual views

The main eye inspected both directions at station 01 on Ronda_Huertas, Calle_Alta_E, Cantabra, Llano, Ribera, San_Pedro, San_Pedro_Salida, Capitan_Salida, Espina_E, El_Sol, Ronda_Mirador and Cervantes (24 actual JPGs). They are views of street composition, not 24 distinct defective buildings.

The census is not an object-level truth. Most sampled fronts already have windows, eaves, cables, boxes, joinery, masonry bases or balconies. Cantabra and El_Sol are already particularly dense. Several supplied descriptions do not match their named JPGs. The flat surfaces actually seen are principally rendered ends in Ronda_Mirador rev01, Llano rev01 and the lower end at Cervantes fwd01. The Ribera fwd01 doorway shows a flat room through glazing, not an open, enterable portal.

| Street family | Recipe | Restraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Huertas / alta / residential | Large exposed rendered ends: occasional accessible lower-storey repaint of the same hue; old window sills get sparse run-off; unrepainted old plain ground bays can retain a repair | No new opening, no forest of downpipes, no random wear on new houses |
| Ribera | Same side-wall treatment when physically exposed; inspect the glazed portal separately | Stone channel fabric and lighting retained |
| Commercial / bars | Existing contemporary density generally already sufficient; intervene only on a large rendered face with few existing layers | Do not make dense Cantabra/Espina a clutter kit |
| All stone / landmarks / confined sides | No new recipe | Respect identity, legitimate blank walls and inaccessible party gaps |

Threshold: at least 18 m² of plain rendered bays; repaint at 24 m². A stable dedicated hash reserves 18% of eligible buildings clean. Repaint is one campaign per building and only on a fraction of candidates. There is no mandate to add every layer to every house. New draws occur after all old RNG draws and use stable id/seed/face/floor/salt hashing.

This is a visual hypothesis to test, not a claimed improvement or independent acceptance.
